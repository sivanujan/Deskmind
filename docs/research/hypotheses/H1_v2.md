## 1. Background & Hypothesis Rationale

In Phase 10 (TRAIN split), H1 v1.0.0 achieved positive net expectancy (+0.0622R) and outranked 94.3% of 1,000 backtest-level random baseline seeds. However, market orders incurred taker fee drag (0.05% notional) plus adverse entry slippage (mean total cost ~0.0366R per trade).

**Hypothesis H1 v2.0.0** tests whether capturing maker rebates/lower fees (0.02% maker rate) and eliminating entry slippage via passive limit orders placed at the OB/FVG proximal edge improves net expectancy without deteriorating fill execution.

---

## 2. Shared Limit-Order Execution & Honest Fill Model

All variants of H1 v2.0.0 adhere to the following execution invariants:

1. **Limit Placement**:
   - Order placed at the **proximal edge** of the 15m Order Block (OB) or Fair Value Gap (FVG) identified at signal bar close $T$.
   - **Bullish (Long)**: `limit_price = current_close - (dist_to_zone * ATR_1h)` (upper boundary of the discount zone). If already inside zone, `limit_price = current_close`.
   - **Bearish (Short)**: `limit_price = current_close + (dist_to_zone * ATR_1h)` (lower boundary of the premium zone). If already inside zone, `limit_price = current_close`.
   - Rounded to exchange `tick_size`.

2. **Honest Fill Rule (Strict Trade-Through Required)**:
   - **Touch-only is NEVER a fill**: If a bar merely touches the limit price (`candle.low == limit_price` for long, or `candle.high == limit_price` for short), queue priority is assumed unfulfilled.
   - **Trade-through by $\ge 1$ tick**:
     - **Long limit fill**: Requires a subsequent 5m bar with $\text{low} \le \text{limit\_price} - \text{tick\_size}$.
     - **Short limit fill**: Requires a subsequent 5m bar with $\text{high} \ge \text{limit\_price} + \text{tick\_size}$.
   - **Fill Price & Fees**: Filled exactly at `limit_price`. Maker fee rate is applied (`0.0002` = 0.02%), with zero entry slippage (`slippage = 0.0`).

3. **Order Expiry ($N$ Bars)**:
   - Order validity window is fixed in advance at **$N = 12$ bars** (60 minutes on 5m timeframe).
   - If the limit order does not fill within 12 bars after generation, it expires and is cancelled.
   - All expired orders are recorded and reported as **missed fills** (`missed_fills`).

4. **Stop-Loss and Take-Profit Geometry**:
   - Stop and target are set **from the actual fill price**:
     - Stop distance: $1.0R = 1.5 \times \text{ATR}_{1h}$.
     - Target distance: $2.0R = 3.0 \times \text{ATR}_{1h}$ ($2:1$ Reward-to-Risk).
     - **Long**: $\text{Stop} = \text{Fill} - 1.5 \times \text{ATR}$, $\text{Target} = \text{Fill} + 3.0 \times \text{ATR}$.
     - **Short**: $\text{Stop} = \text{Fill} + 1.5 \times \text{ATR}$, $\text{Target} = \text{Fill} - 3.0 \times \text{ATR}$.

## 3. Pre-Registered Variants (Maximum 3)

### Variant A: Base Proximal Limit Entry (`H1_v2.0.0_A`)
* **Hypothesis ID**: `H1_v2.0.0_A`
* **Signal Condition**:
  * 1h Premium/Discount: Bullish requires `pd_zone_1h == "discount"`; Bearish requires `pd_zone_1h == "premium"`.
  * Fresh 15m POI: `(fvg_dist <= 1.0 and fvg_size >= 0.5)` OR `(ob_dist <= 1.0 and ob_state == "fresh")`.
  * 5m Trigger: `last_event_5m == "CHoCH_up"` (bullish) or `"CHoCH_down"` (bearish).
* **Execution**: Limit entry at proximal edge; $N=12$ bar trade-through fill rule.
* **Trend Constraint**: None (allows both trend continuation and HTF structural discount reversals).

### Variant B: Proximal Limit + 1h Trend Filter (`H1_v2.0.0_B`)
* **Hypothesis ID**: `H1_v2.0.0_B`
* **Signal Condition**:
  * All conditions of Variant A, PLUS:
  * **1h Trend Direction Filter**:
    * Bullish requires `htf_trend_1h == "bullish"`.
    * Bearish requires `htf_trend_1h == "bearish"`.
* **Execution**: Limit entry at proximal edge; $N=12$ bar trade-through fill rule.
* **Rationale**: Filters out counter-trend structural discount retests, trading strictly with higher timeframe momentum.

### Variant C: Proximal Limit + London/NY Killzones (`H1_v2.0.0_C`)
* **Hypothesis ID**: `H1_v2.0.0_C`
* **Signal Condition**:
  * All conditions of Variant A, PLUS:
  * **ICT Killzone Filter**:
    * Trigger timestamp must fall strictly inside `london_open` (02:00–05:00 NY) or `ny_am` (07:00–10:00 NY) killzone (`killzone in ("london_open", "ny_am")`).
* **Execution**: Limit entry at proximal edge; $N=12$ bar trade-through fill rule.
* **Rationale**: Institutional orderflow volume is concentrated during London and New York opening hours; eliminates Asian session consolidation whipsaws.
