## 1. Background & Hypothesis Rationale

**Hypothesis H6_v1.0.0** (4h Trend Breakout) tests whether classic Donchian channel breakouts on multi-hour timeframes exhibit durable momentum alpha in liquid crypto futures when aligned with higher-timeframe trend direction.

Breakouts on 5m or 15m timeframes frequently suffer from noise, false sweeps, and high fee drag. By aggregating 5m klines into strictly closed 4h bars, H6 tests whether 20-period 4h range breakouts with confirmed EMA50 slope filter can capture macro trend expansions with favorable risk-to-reward geometry.

## 2. Bar Construction & Indicator Definitions

### 2.1 4h Bar Resampling (Strictly CLOSED Bars, No Look-Ahead)
- **Timeframe**: 4h bars resampled from underlying 5m klines.
- **Alignment**: Standard UTC 4-hour boundaries: `00:00`, `04:00`, `08:00`, `12:00`, `16:00`, `20:00` UTC (`timestamp % 14_400_000 == 0`).
- **Resampling Aggregation**:
  - `open`: Open of the first 5m bar in the 4h window.
  - `high`: Maximum high of all 5m bars in the 4h window.
  - `low`: Minimum low of all 5m bars in the 4h window.
  - `close`: Close of the 48th (final) 5m bar in the 4h window.
  - `volume`: Sum of volumes across all 5m bars in the 4h window.
  - `open_time`: Start of the 4h window.
  - `close_time`: End of the 4h window (`open_time + 14_400_000`).
- **No Look-Ahead**: At any time $T$, only 4h bars with $\text{close\_time} \le T$ are considered closed and available for calculation. Ongoing (unclosed) 4h bars are strictly excluded.

### 2.2 Indicators on 4h Closed Bars
1. **20-Bar Channel High / Low**:
   - For bar index $t$, the previous 20 4h bars span indices $[t-20, t-1]$.
   - $\text{High}_{20} = \max(\text{high}_{t-20}, \dots, \text{high}_{t-1})$
   - $\text{Low}_{20} = \min(\text{low}_{t-20}, \dots, \text{low}_{t-1})$
2. **EMA50(4h) and Slope**:
   - 50-period Exponential Moving Average of 4h close prices:
     $$\alpha = \frac{2}{50 + 1} = \frac{2}{51}$$
   - **Rising EMA50**: $\text{EMA50}_t > \text{EMA50}_{t-5}$ (EMA50 on closed bar $t$ strictly greater than EMA50 5 4h bars ago).
   - **Falling EMA50**: $\text{EMA50}_t < \text{EMA50}_{t-5}$.
3. **ATR14(4h)**:
   - 14-period Average True Range on 4h bars:
     $$\text{TR}_t = \max(\text{high}_t - \text{low}_t, |\text{high}_t - \text{close}_{t-1}|, |\text{low}_t - \text{close}_{t-1}|)$$
     $$\text{ATR}_t = \frac{\text{ATR}_{t-1} \times 13 + \text{TR}_t}{14}$$

## 3. Signal Conditions & Breakout State Machine

### 3.1 Long Breakout Rule
A Bullish Breakout signal triggers on the close of 4h bar $t$ if and only if:
1. $\text{close}_t > \text{High}_{20} = \max(\text{high}_{t-20}, \dots, \text{high}_{t-1})$.
2. $\text{EMA50}_t > \text{EMA50}_{t-5}$ (EMA50 is rising).
3. The symbol is **not currently in a long breakout** (one signal per breakout).

### 3.2 Short Breakout Rule
A Bearish Breakout signal triggers on the close of 4h bar $t$ if and only if:
1. $\text{close}_t < \text{Low}_{20} = \min(\text{low}_{t-20}, \dots, \text{low}_{t-1})$.
2. $\text{EMA50}_t < \text{EMA50}_{t-5}$ (EMA50 is falling).
3. The symbol is **not currently in a short breakout** (one signal per breakout).

### 3.3 One Signal per Breakout (State Machine)
- When a breakout triggers, the symbol enters `in_breakout = True` for that direction.
- Subsequent 4h bars that remain above the previous 20-bar high are ignored; no re-entry signals are emitted.
- The breakout state resets only when a 4h bar closes back inside the range ($\text{close} \le \text{High}_{20}$ for long, $\text{close} \ge \text{Low}_{20}$ for short). Only after this reset can a new 20-bar high or low trigger a fresh signal.

## 4. Execution, Risk Geometry & Exit Rules

1. **Market Entry**:
   - Order type: **Market order** executed at the open of the very next 5m bar immediately following the 4h bar close.
   - Entry fee: Binance Futures standard taker fee (`0.0005` / 5 bps) + adverse slippage.

2. **Stop Loss & Target Geometry**:
   - Stop Distance: $1.0R = 2.0 \times \text{ATR14}(4\text{h})$.
   - Target Distance: $2.0R = 4.0 \times \text{ATR14}(4\text{h})$ ($2:1$ Reward-to-Risk).
   - **Long**: $\text{Stop} = \text{Fill} - 2.0 \times \text{ATR14}(4\text{h})$, $\text{Target} = \text{Fill} + 4.0 \times \text{ATR14}(4\text{h})$.
   - **Short**: $\text{Stop} = \text{Fill} + 2.0 \times \text{ATR14}(4\text{h})$, $\text{Target} = \text{Fill} - 4.0 \times \text{ATR14}(4\text{h})$.
   - Rounded to symbol filters `tick_size`.

3. **Honest Take-Profit (TP) Rule**:
   - TP executes as a Limit order.
   - Fills ONLY if a subsequent bar trades **THROUGH** the target price by $\ge 1$ tick:
     - Long TP: $\text{high} \ge \text{target} + \text{tick\_size}$.
     - Short TP: $\text{low} \le \text{target} - \text{tick\_size}$.
   - Touch-only does NOT fill.
   - Maker fee rate (`0.0002` / 2 bps) with zero slippage.

4. **Time Exit (Max Hold: 10 Days)**:
   - Maximum holding period is **10 days** ($864,000,000\text{ ms} = 2,880 \text{ 5m bars}$).
   - If a position has not hit stop loss or take profit within 10 days from entry, it is closed at market on the next 5m bar open (`exit_reason = "time_limit"`).
   - Executed as Market order $\to$ Taker fee (`0.0005`) + adverse slippage.

5. **Stop Loss (SL) Exit**:
   - Stop executes as a Market order on bar crossing $\to$ Taker fee (`0.0005`) + adverse slippage.
